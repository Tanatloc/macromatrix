Most apps count calories. MacroMatrix shows the whole picture: energy, protein, carbs and fat, plus every vitamin, mineral, fatty acid and essential amino acid. That is 69 nutrients, each compared with official EFSA reference values.

Free and open source, with no ads and no trackers. No account needed: your diary stays on your phone and the app works offline.

<b>Know what you're missing</b>
• A Nutrient Score for every day, week and month
• See at a glance which nutrients are low, fine or too high
• Short lists of real foods that fill today's gaps
• Weekly report with a clear verdict on salt, fibre, sugar, saturated fat and more

<b>Log in seconds</b>
• 38,000+ foods from official food tables (European first), plus millions of branded products
• Scan a barcode, say what you ate, or take a photo of your meal (3 a day free; you check the estimate before it is saved)
• Recipes, saved meals, copy a day, week menu with shopping list

<b>Scan before you buy</b>
• Product score, Nutri-Score, processing level and additives with their EFSA-based risk
• Your allergens and a healthier alternative
• Unknown product? Scan the label (1 a day free) and share it with Open Food Facts if you want

<b>Better with friends (optional)</b>
• Weekly leaderboard, battles, challenges, levels, badges and titles
• Friends only see your name, avatar, level, badges and weekly score. Never your diary, weight or email address
• Block and report built in

<b>Fits your routine</b>
• Health Connect: steps, workouts and weight from your watch or smart scale
• Home screen widget, reminders, backups, light and dark theme
• English and Dutch. Works offline

<b>Free, no ads, privacy first</b>
• Free, and open source under the AGPL: github.com/Tanatloc/macromatrix
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics. Your data is never sold
• No account needed: everything is stored on your phone and works offline
• Want to sync between devices? A free account is optional, and your data is stored in the EU

Supporter is optional (in the Google Play version): €3.99 a month or €29.99 a year, or Duo for you and a friend at €4.99 a month or €39.99 a year. It adds extras such as avatar frames, more colours, more photo and label scans per day, recipe import and early access, and it keeps the servers running without ads.

MacroMatrix is not medical advice.
