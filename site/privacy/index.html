Privacy policy

Version of 24 October 2026

In short. We only store what is needed to make the app work: your account, your profile and what you log. Your data is stored in the EU, other users cannot access it (except friends you add yourself: they only see your name and your weekly score), we sell nothing and show no ads. You can export or permanently delete everything at any time.

Who is responsible?

MacroMatrix is run by DivineNet. For any question about your data, email privacy@macromatrix.eu.

Friends

The friends feature is off until you open it yourself. After that, people you accept (or people you add who accept you) can see your name, your profile photo or avatar, your level, your title, your showcase badges and your average Nutrient Score per week, plus the number of days you logged that week. If you start a battle, your opponent also sees your battle points for that week (the sum of your daily scores). A photo is resized to 192 by 192 pixels before it leaves your device. If you have Pro, they also see a Supporter badge next to your name and the avatar frame you chose (also people in your battles and challenges), unless you turn the badge off on the Pro screen. They never see your email address, your diary, your weight or any other data. You can remove a friend at any time; from then on they see nothing.

You can also block someone. You are then no longer friends, open battles and challenge invites between you disappear, and neither of you can send the other a friend request until you unblock them. They are not told that you blocked them. If someone misbehaves (for example spam, harassment or an inappropriate name or photo), you can report them. We store the report, with the reason, your optional note and the name and photo or avatar they had at that moment, so we can review it and act on it, for example by removing their name or photo or closing their account. The person you report does not see who reported them, and other users cannot see reports. You can send at most 10 reports per day.

Logging with a photo

If you take or choose a photo of your meal in "Quick log", the app first makes it smaller on your device (at most 1024 pixels) and sends it, with your login, to our server on Cloudflare. There an image model (Cloudflare Workers AI) describes the food it sees, and only that text comes back to the app; you check everything before it is added. The photo is not stored. To share the cost of the image model fairly, we count per account how many photos you used that day (UTC): 3 per day are free, and with Pro up to 50. Only that number is stored, in our database in the EU, and old days are removed after a week.

Scanning a label

If a barcode is not found, you can take a photo of the nutrition table (and, if you like, of the front of the pack). The app makes the photos smaller on your device and sends them, with your login, to our server on Cloudflare. There an image model (Cloudflare Workers AI) reads the values, and only those values come back to fill in the form for your own product; you check everything before you save it. The photos are not stored. The product is saved as your own product in your account, like any product you enter yourself. We count per account how many labels you scanned that day (UTC): 1 per day is free, and with Pro up to 30. Only that number is stored.

Sharing a product with Open Food Facts (optional)

After you save a scanned product, the app asks whether you want to share it with Open Food Facts, a free public food database, so others can find it too. Only if you tap Share, our server sends Open Food Facts the barcode, the product name, the brand, the nutrition values per 100 g and your photo of the label (and of the front, if you added one), using the MacroMatrix account at Open Food Facts. Nothing about you is sent: not your name, email address or account. What you share becomes public and is published by Open Food Facts under the Open Database Licence (ODbL), with the photos under Creative Commons BY-SA; it can be edited by others and cannot be withdrawn through us. If you tap No, nothing is sent.

Importing a recipe (Pro)

With Pro you can import a recipe from a link or from a photo of a cookbook page. For a link, our server on Cloudflare fetches that web page itself (the website sees our server, not you) and reads the recipe from it; if the page has no structured recipe, an AI model (Cloudflare Workers AI) reads the text of the page. A photo is made smaller on your device, sent with your login and read by the image model. Only the recipe name, the number of servings and the ingredient lines come back to the app, and you check everything before the recipe is saved. The link, the page and the photo are not stored. We count per account how many recipes you imported that day (up to 20 with Pro); only that number is stored.

Pro

MacroMatrix is free; Pro is an optional subscription for unlimited photo logging and a few extras for supporters. For Pro we store until when it runs, where it came from, whether your Supporter badge is shown to friends and which avatar frame you chose.

On Android you can subscribe through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment itself, under its own privacy policy; we never see your card details, name or address from Google. We receive and store the purchase token of your subscription, which product it is (monthly or yearly) and until when it is paid, linked to your account. Our server uses the token to check your subscription with Google Play, also when Google tells us it was renewed, cancelled or refunded. So that Google can check that a purchase belongs to your account, the app sends Google a scrambled code made from your account ID (not your email address). The purchase data is deleted together with your account; your purchase history at Google stays with Google. Cancel a subscription in the Play Store (Payments and subscriptions); deleting your account does not cancel it.

With Pro you can vote on the roadmap in "What's next" (at most 3 votes at a time). Your votes are stored linked to your account, so you can see and take back your own votes, and they are deleted together with your account. Other users only see how many votes an item has, never who voted. If your Pro ends, your votes stay stored but no longer count. Which new features are in early access for you follows from your Pro status; nothing extra is stored for that.

Health Connect (Android)

Only if you turn it on in the settings, MacroMatrix reads your steps, workouts (with the energy burned and distance), weight and, if a smart scale provides them, body fat, lean body mass and body water from Health Connect on your phone, for the last 7 days. They become measurements and exercise in your diary, just as if you had entered them yourself, and are stored and synced like the rest of your data. MacroMatrix never writes to Health Connect and does not use this data for anything else: it is never sold, shared with others or used for advertising. You can turn it off at any time in the settings or in Health Connect.

Battle notifications (Android)

To notify you when a friend challenges you, your phone gets a random device code. About every 15 minutes, your phone uses that code to ask our database whether there are new challenges for you; the answer only contains the challenger's name. The code is removed when you log out or delete your account.

Voice, reminders and the widget

If you use "Quick log" with your voice, the speech recognition of your device or browser (on Android usually Google's) turns your words into text. MacroMatrix only receives that text and does not store any audio. On phones without their own speech recognition (for example GrapheneOS), the app records up to 15 seconds itself and sends that recording, only when you tap the microphone, to our server on Cloudflare, where Whisper (Cloudflare Workers AI) turns it into text. Only the text comes back; the recording is not stored. Reminders are scheduled on your device itself; nothing is sent to our server for that. The home screen widget only shows data that is already on your phone.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent out your data.
  • We do not show ads and do not use your data for marketing.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or analytics on the website or in the app.
  • We do not make decisions about you based on automated profiling.

How long do we keep it?

As long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your account and all your data are deleted right away. Copies in backups disappear within 30 days. An account you never confirmed is deleted after 30 days. A report is kept as long as we need it to handle the abuse, and is deleted when you or the person you reported delete their account.

Security

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The database only lets each user see and change their own data.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: in the app via Profile → Settings → Export diary (CSV). If you want everything, email us.
  • Correction: you can change everything yourself in the app.
  • Deletion: Profile → Settings → Delete account, also in the web app. See how to delete your account, also without the app.
  • Objection and restriction: email us; we reply within a month.
  • Complaint: with the Belgian Data Protection Authority, dataprotectionauthority.be, or the authority in your own EU country.

Age

The app is meant for adults (18 or older). The reference values do not apply to children, nor during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Changes

If we change this policy, you will see it in the date at the top. For important changes, we will let you know in the app.
