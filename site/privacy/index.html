Privacy policy

Version of 17 October 2026

In short. We only store what is needed to make the app work: your account, your profile and what you log. Your data is stored in the EU, other users cannot access it (except friends you add yourself: they only see your name and your weekly score), we sell nothing and show no ads. You can export or permanently delete everything at any time.

Who is responsible?

MacroMatrix is run by DivineNet. For any question about your data, email privacy@divinenet.online.

Some of this is health data. We only process it because you explicitly consent to it when you create your account (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR), and only to make the app work for you. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account.

Friends

The friends feature is off until you open it yourself. After that, people you accept (or people you add who accept you) can see your name, your profile photo or avatar, your level, your title, your showcase badges and your average Nutrient Score per week, plus the number of days you logged that week. If you start a battle, your opponent also sees your battle points for that week (the sum of your daily scores). A photo is resized to 192 by 192 pixels before it leaves your device. They never see your email address, your diary, your weight or any other data. You can remove a friend at any time; from then on they see nothing.

Health Connect (Android)

Only if you turn it on in the settings, MacroMatrix reads your steps, workouts (with the energy burned and distance) and weight from Health Connect on your phone, for the last 7 days. They become measurements and exercise in your diary, just as if you had entered them yourself, and are stored and synced like the rest of your data. MacroMatrix never writes to Health Connect and does not use this data for anything else. You can turn it off at any time in the settings or in Health Connect.

Battle notifications (Android)

To notify you when a friend challenges you, your phone gets a random device code. About every 15 minutes, your phone uses that code to ask our database whether there are new challenges for you; the answer only contains the challenger's name. The code is removed when you log out or delete your account.

Voice, reminders and the widget

If you use "Quick log" with your voice, the speech recognition of your device or browser (on Android usually Google's) turns your words into text. MacroMatrix only receives that text and does not store any audio. On phones without their own speech recognition (for example GrapheneOS), the app records up to 15 seconds itself and sends that recording, only when you tap the microphone, to our server on Cloudflare, where Whisper (Cloudflare Workers AI) turns it into text. Only the text comes back; the recording is not stored. Reminders are scheduled on your device itself; nothing is sent to our server for that. The home screen widget only shows data that is already on your phone.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent out your data.
  • We do not show ads and do not use your data for marketing.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or analytics on the website or in the app.
  • We do not make decisions about you based on automated profiling.

How long do we keep it?

As long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your account and all your data are deleted right away. Copies in backups disappear within 30 days. An account you never confirmed is deleted after 30 days.

Security

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The database only lets each user see and change their own data.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: in the app via Profile → Settings → Export diary (CSV). If you want everything, email us.
  • Correction: you can change everything yourself in the app.
  • Deletion: Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  • Objection and restriction: email us; we reply within a month.
  • Complaint: with the Belgian Data Protection Authority, dataprotectionauthority.be, or the authority in your own EU country.

Age

The app is meant for adults (18 or older). The reference values do not apply to children, nor during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Changes

If we change this policy, you will see it in the date at the top. For important changes, we will let you know in the app.
