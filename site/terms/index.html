1. What MacroMatrix is

MacroMatrix is a free food diary by VanoWeb. You log what you eat and see your energy, macros, vitamins and minerals compared with general reference values. By creating an account, you accept these terms and the privacy policy.

2. Not medical advice

The app gives information, not medical or dietary advice. The targets are general EFSA reference values for healthy adults and do not apply to children, during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or with a medical condition. Talk to your doctor or dietitian before you make big changes to your diet or supplements.

3. Accuracy of the data

Nutrition values come from public databases (ANSES-CIQUAL, Livsmedelsverket, Matvaretabellen, USDA) and from Open Food Facts, which is filled in by its users. Additive risk levels are based on EFSA and ANSES assessments as recorded in Open Food Facts. Some values are estimated and marked with ≈. We do our best, but cannot guarantee that all values are correct or complete. Allergy warnings are a help, not a guarantee: if you have an allergy, always check the label.

It mainly helps pay for the servers and the AI scans.

Duo is the same subscription for two people: the buyer chooses one friend (someone who accepted their friend request) who gets the supporter extras too, for as long as the buyer's Duo runs. The buyer can change that friend at most once every 30 days; the friend can leave at any time. When the Duo ends, the friend's extras end too.

You buy Supporter and Duo through Google Play. The subscription renews automatically every month or year at the price shown before you confirm, until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions; you keep Supporter until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. Deleting your MacroMatrix account does not cancel a Google Play subscription, so cancel it there as well.
