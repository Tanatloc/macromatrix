Made in the EU or the US?

Better choices

A low score? You get two or three similar products that score better, like whole-grain flakes instead of frosted cereal.

Unknown barcode? Scan the label

Take a photo of the nutrition table and AI fills in the values. Check them, because it can misread a blurry label. Then, if you like, share the product with Open Food Facts so the next person finds it. 1 label a day is free.

Privacy

What happens to your data

What you eat and weigh is personal. No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold or used for anything other than running the app.

Stored in the EU

Your account and data are stored inside the EU, on Cloudflare's servers. The emails with your code are sent through Cloudflare too, with the French service Brevo as a backup.

Only you see your diary

Friends only see your name, level, badges and weekly score. Nobody else can access your data.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no tracking cookies and no analytics, on the website or in the app. We never sell or rent out your data.

Photos are not stored
Meal and label photos are read by AI on our server and then thrown away, unless you choose to share a label with Open Food Facts. We only keep a count of how many you used that day, for a week at most.
Backed up every night
With an account, your data is copied every night into an encrypted backup in the EU. Once a month we check that a backup can really be restored. Backups are kept for 30 days.
Delete at any time
One button in the settings deletes your account and all your data for good. Backups are gone within 30 days.
Open source

All of the app's code is public under the AGPL licence. You don't have to take our word for any of the above: you can read what the app does with your data.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes, and there is no paywall. An account is free too, and optional. Supporter is an optional subscription (€3.99 a month or €29.99 a year) with a few extras like avatar frames, colours and more AI scans per day. It helps pay for the AI and the servers. It is coming soon through Google Play.

What is Duo?

Duo is Supporter for two: €4.99 a month or €39.99 a year. You pay, and you pick one of your friends in the app who gets all the Supporter extras too, for as long as your Duo runs. You can switch to another friend once every 30 days. Your friend can leave the Duo at any time.

Are there ads? Do you sell my data?

No and no. There are no ads, no tracking cookies and no analytics. Your data is stored in the EU and is never sold or used for anything other than running the app. See the privacy policy.

Is MacroMatrix open source?

Yes. The code is public under the AGPL licence on GitHub.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes, in the Android app and in the browser. Choose "Use without an account" on the start screen. Everything then stays on that device only, or in that browser if you use the web version. Clearing your browsing data or using a private window wipes it. Without an account there is no sync, no friends or battles, and no logging with a photo. You can create an account later in Settings and what you logged comes along.

What do my friends see?

Your chosen name, your avatar or photo, your level, title and showcase badges, your average Nutrient Score per week and how many days you logged. In a battle they also see your points for that week, and in a challenge whether you made each day. If you are a supporter, they see your Supporter badge and avatar frame. Never your diary, your weight or your email address.

How do battles work?

You challenge a friend for a week. Every day you log, your Nutrient Score for that day adds to your points. Whoever has the most points on Sunday wins. Wins count towards your battle badges and your rivalry record with that friend.

How is the product score calculated?

60% comes from the Nutri-Score, 30% from the additives (based on EFSA assessments) and 10% from whether the product is organic. A product with a high-risk additive scores 49 at most.

Which devices does it work on?

In any modern browser, on your computer and on your phone. The Android app (barcode scanner, widget, Health Connect) can be downloaded here; the Play Store is coming soon.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything is also stored on your device, including products you scanned before. With an account, what you log offline is sent once you are back online.

How accurate are the nutrition values?

Basic foods come from official food composition tables. Branded products come from Open Food Facts, which is filled in by its users, and USDA; check the label if in doubt. Estimated values are always marked with ≈.

Is this medical advice?

No. The targets are general EFSA reference values for healthy adults. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding or have a medical condition, talk to your doctor or dietitian.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account, or see this page. Your account and all your data are deleted right away.

How do I contact you?

Email support@macromatrix.eu for help, or privacy@macromatrix.eu for questions about your data.
