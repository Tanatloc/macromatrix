FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes. An account is free too, and optional.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes. Choose "Use without an account" on the start screen. Everything then stays on your device only, without sync, friends or battles. You can create an account later; your data comes along.

What do my friends see?

Your chosen name, your avatar or photo, your level, title and showcase badges, and your average Nutrient Score per week. In a battle they also see your points for that week. Never your diary, your weight or your email address.

How do battles work?

You challenge a friend for a week. Every day you log, your Nutrient Score for that day adds to your points. Whoever has the most points on Sunday wins. Wins count towards your battle badges and your rivalry record with that friend.

How is the product score calculated?

60% comes from the Nutri-Score, 30% from the additives (based on EFSA assessments) and 10% from whether the product is organic. A product with a high-risk additive scores 49 at most.

Which devices does it work on?

In any modern browser, on your computer and on your phone. The Android app (barcode scanner, widget, Health Connect) can be downloaded from GitHub; the Play Store is coming soon.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After you log in, everything is also stored on your device, including products you scanned before. What you log offline goes to your account once you are back online.

How accurate are the nutrition values?

Basic foods come from official food composition tables. Branded products come from Open Food Facts, which is filled in by its users, and USDA; check the label if in doubt. Estimated values are always marked with ≈.

Is this medical advice?

No. The targets are general EFSA reference values for healthy adults. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding or have a medical condition, talk to your doctor or dietitian.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your account and all your data are deleted right away.
